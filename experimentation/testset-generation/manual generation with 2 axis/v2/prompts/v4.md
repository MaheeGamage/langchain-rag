You are building an evaluation dataset for a RAG system that assists quantum software 
developers with experiment tracking using MLflow and QProv standards.

The system's users are quantum researchers and software engineers who may be unfamiliar 
with experiment tracking tooling.

The questions must sound like something a real quantum developer would type into a 
chat interface.

Generate 4 question and answer pairs based on below categorization and below subdomain.

Subdomain: Qiskit-Specific Experiment Tracking Using MLflow and QProv

Categorization:

- **fact_single**
  - **Description:** Answer is present in the context. It has one unit of information and cannot be partially correct.
  - **Example Context:** A table of a sensor's electrical properties
  - **Example Query:** What supply voltage should I use?

- **summary**
  - **Description:** Answer is present in the context. It has multiple units of information. Trading completeness for conciseness yields a partially correct answer.
  - **Example Context:** The conclusion section of a paper
  - **Example Query:** Summarize their key findings for me

- **reasoning**
  - **Description:** Answer is not explicitly mentioned in the context but can be inferred from it via simple reasoning.
  - **Example Context:** An ESG report section on a company's electricity usage
  - **Example Query:** Has there been a net increase in consumption over 5 years?

- **unanswerable**
  - **Description:** Answer is neither present in the context nor can be inferred from it.
  - **Example Context:** Claims from a patent on a coffee machine
  - **Example Query:** Is tomato a fruit or a vegetable?

---

## Answer Production Rules

These rules apply to all categories. Write every answer as plain prose sentences with no bullet points, headers, or special formatting.

- **fact_single:** State the single fact in one sentence. Do not explain or justify it.
- **summary:** Write two to four sentences covering only the key facts. Omit background, context, and elaboration.
- **reasoning:** State the conclusion directly in one to two sentences. Do not narrate the reasoning steps that led to it.
- **unanswerable:** Write one sentence stating that the information is not available in the provided sources. Do not describe what the sources do contain.
