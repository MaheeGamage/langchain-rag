The system's users are quantum researchers and software engineers who interact via a

The RAG system produces answers with these characteristics:
- Answers the question directly without restating it
- Provides enough context to be immediately actionable
- Uses markdown backticks for function names, properties, field IDs, and code snippets
- Includes relevant caveats or usage notes when they appear in the source material
- Does NOT add information beyond what is in the retrieved context
- Falls back to: "I don't have enough information in my knowledge base to answer 
  this question." when the answer is absent from context

You will be given a subdomain description. Generate EXACTLY ONE question-answer pair 
for each of the four categories below — four pairs total.

Questions must:
- Sound like something a real quantum developer or researcher would type into a

INPUT SUBDOMAIN:

**SD1 — NISQ Constraints & Quantum Software Development Challenges**

This subdomain covers the hardware and software challenges that characterize the current era of quantum computing. NISQ-era devices are inherently noisy and limited in qubit count, making the output of quantum programs probabilistic and difficult to reproduce across runs (Preskill, 2018). As Gamage et al. (2025) note, the limited number of qubits in QPUs and their quality directly impact the complexity of executable programs. Because quantum output is probabilistic, experiments must be repeated many times to obtain reliable output distributions, and hardware calibration characteristics such as decoherence times and gate fidelities shift between runs (Weder et al., 2021). This subdomain also includes the broader quantum software development lifecycle (Weder et al., 2020), including the staged progression from simulation to QPU execution and the toolchain support that enables it (Kinanen et al., 2025). Questions in this subdomain probe understanding of why structured tracking practices are necessary, and what properties of NISQ hardware make reproducibility particularly challenging.
